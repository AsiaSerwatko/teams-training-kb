# Schedule class sessions and set meeting options

Schedule sessions from the team's channel, not from your personal calendar. Teams then saves recordings in the team's files, where you can find them for every group in the same place.

**Applies to:** Microsoft Teams (desktop and web)  
**Time needed:** about 10 minutes per group

## Before you begin

- The group has a class team. See [Create a class team for a training group](02-create-class-team.md).
- All trainers have accounts in your organization. Only people from your organization who are invited to a meeting can become its co-organizers.

## Schedule the sessions

1. Open the team. Teams opens the **General** channel.
2. In the upper-right corner, select the arrow next to **Meet**, and then select **Schedule a meeting**.
   <!-- Screenshot: Meet > Schedule a meeting in the channel header -->
3. Enter a title, the dates and the times. If the group meets regularly, set the meeting to repeat.
4. Under **Required attendees**, add every trainer who leads at least one session.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Add a trainer to the whole series, even if they lead only some of the sessions. You can make someone a co-organizer only if they are invited to the meeting.

5. Select **Send**. The sessions appear in your Teams calendar.

## Edit one session or the whole series

Select a session in your Teams calendar, and then choose one of these options:

| Option | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Edit occurrence** | Change the time of a single session, or invite a guest who won't co-organize. |
| **Edit series** | Turn on automatic recording, or add someone who will be a co-organizer. |

In the scheduling form, select **Meeting options** to open the settings described in the next section.

## Set meeting options

On the **Meeting options** page, set the following:

1. **Who can bypass the lobby?** For dedicated client groups, select **Everyone** so that participants join without waiting in the lobby.
2. **Who can present.** Usually **Everyone**, so that participants can share their screens during exercises.
3. **Choose co-organizers.** Select the trainers. You can choose up to 10 co-organizers.
   <!-- Screenshot: Choose co-organizers with two trainers selected -->

> [!NOTE]
> You don't appear in this list: whoever schedules the meeting is its organizer. If the list is empty, nobody eligible is invited yet. Edit the series and add the trainers under **Required attendees**.

4. **Record and transcribe automatically.** Select **Record and transcribe**, or **Record only** if your organization offers it.

> [!NOTE]
> This setting used to be a simple on/off switch. Teams now shows a list of options, and which ones you see depends on your organization's meeting policies.

5. Select **Save** at the bottom of the page. You can now close the browser tab.

## Troubleshooting

**I can't find a trainer when I add attendees or co-organizers.**
The trainer probably doesn't have an account in your organization yet. See [Add a trainer to your Microsoft 365 organization](01-add-trainer-to-organization.md).

**The co-organizer list is empty.**
Edit the **series**, not a single occurrence, and add the trainers as required attendees.
